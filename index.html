How It Works

Understand how GPA is calculated and how to make the most of your studies.

What is SGPA?

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of grade points earned in a single semester, weighted by credit hours. It reflects your performance for that term only.

How is SGPA calculated?

For each subject, multiply credit hours by the grade point to get Quality Points, then divide by total credit hours:

What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the overall average across all semesters, calculated using the credit weighted average of each semester's SGPA not a simple average GPAs

How is CGPA calculated?

What are credit hours?

Credit hours represent the weight of a course based on lecture/lab time per week. Higher credit courses have a larger impact on your GPA.

SGPA vs. CGPA difference?

SGPA measures a single semester; CGPA measures your entire academic career. High-credit courses shift your CGPA more than smaller ones.

How to improve your GPA?

Focus first on courses with higher credit hours and lower grades. The Improvement Tools let you test scenarios before retaking classes.
